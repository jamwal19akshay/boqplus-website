This guide shows how to install BOQ+ on an Android phone or an iPhone, how to sign in, and what the setup asks you after your first sign-in. The details are as of 8 November 2026.

## BOQ app download for Android and iPhone

BOQ+ is on Google Play for Android phones and on the App Store for iPhone. Both store badges are on the [BOQ+ home page](/). Use the badge for your phone to reach the store listing.

### Android

Use the Google Play badge on the home page to open the BOQ+ listing, then install the app from there. Google Play controls the listing wording and availability.

### iPhone

Use the App Store badge on the home page to open the BOQ+ listing, then install the app from there. The App Store controls the listing wording and availability.

## Signing in to BOQ+

The sign-in options depend on your phone:

- **Android:** you sign in with Google.
- **iPhone:** you can sign in with Apple or with Google.

The app has no email, password or phone-number (OTP) sign-in. Whichever phone you use, choose from the options above.

Signing in saves your BOQs to your account. This means they follow you to another phone.

### Look around before you sign in

You can look around without signing in. The sign-in screen has an 'Explore without signing in' option. On later sign-in prompts, the same choice is shown as 'Continue as Guest'.

Uploading a BOQ needs a signed-in account. If you are a guest and try to upload, the app asks you to sign in at that point.

When a guest signs in, the guest's BOQs and free-preview count are carried into the account. So anything you did as a guest is not left behind.

### Setup questions after first sign-in

After your first sign-in, the setup asks three short questions:

- which state you take contracts in
- which departments you bid with
- which city you work in most

The setup screens and plan screens can be shown in English or Hindi.

## What to do after you install

Here is a simple order for a first-time user:

1. Install the app from the store for your phone, using the badge on the home page.
2. Look around as a guest to see how the screens work.
3. Sign in when you are ready to upload a BOQ. Use Google on Android, or Apple or Google on iPhone.
4. Answer the three setup questions about your state, departments and city.
5. Upload your BOQ.

Because signing in saves your BOQs to your account, they follow you if you move to another phone.

Once your file is in the app, the next step is pricing it. The guide [How to Price a BOQ in BOQ+: Step-by-Step on Your Phone](/blog/price-e-tender-boq-file-in-boq-plus) covers that. If you are new to BOQ files themselves, read [how to fill a BOQ for an e-tender](/blog/how-to-fill-boq-government-e-tender). The [FAQ](/faq) has answers to common questions about the app. For the rate books and data behind the app, see the [sources page](/sources).

## Deleting your account

Account deletion is available inside the app under Settings. There is also a delete-account page on boq.co.in.

## Things to know before you install

- Screens and button names may change between app versions, so the labels you see may not match this guide exactly.
- Sign-in options differ by phone. Android has Google only. iPhone has Apple and Google.
- Store listing wording and availability are controlled by Google Play and the App Store, not by BOQ+.

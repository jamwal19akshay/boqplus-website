This article shows how to check an item rate in the Prices tab of BOQ+ and explains what the numbers are. The figures described are as of 20 November 2026.

The Prices tab shows one CPWD reference rate for each listed item. Each rate is a Delhi figure built from published CPWD documents. It is not a quote and it is not a local market price.

## Where to find the BOQ price list in the app

Prices is a sub-tab inside the BidIQ tab. It sits next to Intelligence, Rates and Calculators. It appears only when BOQ+ has switched it on. If you do not see it, it may not be switched on yet.

### What the list looks like

The Prices list is one list of CPWD reference rates for Delhi. Every item is labelled "CPWD reference rate". There is no city or state picker, so you cannot switch the list to another place. You can search the list by item name.

For general questions about BOQ+, see the [BOQ+ FAQ](/faq).

## How to check an item's rate

1. Open the BidIQ tab and tap Prices.
2. Search the list by item name.
3. Tap the item to see how its rate is built.
4. Read the base rate, the time factor, and the CPWD documents the rate comes from.
5. Open the "How this is calculated" link to read the method.

## What the numbers are built from

Each price is the CPWD Delhi Schedule of Rates 2023 (Vol 1) rate multiplied by a time factor. The time factor brings the rate up to the latest CPWD Delhi Building Cost Index.

The index in use is the CPWD Delhi Building Cost Index of 101 as on 1 Apr 2026. It is listed with its CPWD circular on the [Data sources page](/sources).

So when you open an item, you are looking at two parts:

- The base rate, which comes from the CPWD Delhi Schedule of Rates 2023 (Vol 1).
- The time factor, which the base rate is multiplied by to reach the latest index.

For more on the index, read [how the CPWD cost index updates rates](/blog/cpwd-cost-index-explained). For the schedule itself, read [what the CPWD DSR 2023 holds](/blog/cpwd-dsr-2023-explained).

## One rate, not a range

Each item shows one rate. It is not a minimum-to-maximum range. Do not read it as a spread of prices from different places or suppliers. It is a single reference figure from CPWD documents for Delhi, moved forward by the time factor.

## Reading the header and disclaimer

The header says: "Delhi reference rates from CPWD. Local prices may differ." The disclaimer says: "Indicative. Not a quotation."

The method text says these are government cost indices applied to Delhi reference rates. They are not quotes from suppliers. It advises you to confirm with local suppliers before bidding.

## What a contractor can do with this information

- Use the list as a starting check, not as your final rate.
- Open the build-up and see how much of the figure comes from the base rate and how much from the time factor.
- Compare the Delhi rate with your own supplier quotes for the same item.
- Confirm prices with local suppliers before you bid, as the method text advises.
- Check the index month shown for the list when you prepare a bid, because it changes when BOQ+ loads a new CPWD index reading.
- Keep a dated record of the quotes you used.

The Delhi reference does not reflect your location. Your own rate depends on your local prices, so treat the list as one input among several. ## If the list does not load

The Prices list is loaded from BOQ+ over the internet. If a refresh fails, the app shows the last list it saved. If no list was ever loaded, it shows an error with a Retry option.

## Limits of these figures

- Screens and button names may change between app versions.
- These are Delhi reference rates built from published CPWD documents. They are not live market quotes and do not reflect your location.
- The index month and numbers change when BOQ+ loads a new CPWD index reading.
- Items without an official CPWD base rate are not in the list.

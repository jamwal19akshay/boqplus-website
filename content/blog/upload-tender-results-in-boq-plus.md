This guide explains how to add a tender result to BOQ+, which file to upload, and what the app does with it. The steps and screens described here are as of 23 November 2026.

## How to add a tender result in the BOQ app

You can add a result from two places in the app:

- The BidIQ tab, using 'Add a previous tender result'.
- My Track Record, using 'Add a tender result'.

Adding a result is optional. BidIQ analysis is ready either way. A result from a similar tender gives the analysis extra context to compare against. For background on the report itself, read [BOQ App BidIQ Report: What It Shows and What It Does Not Claim](/blog/bidiq-report-explained).

## Which file to upload

### The BOQ Comparative Chart

The file to upload is the BOQ Comparative Chart, downloaded from the tender portal. It must be an Excel file, either .xls or .xlsx, and up to 10 MB in size.

### Finding the chart on a GePNIC portal

On a GePNIC portal, the app points you to this path:

1. Tender Status
2. Financial Evaluation
3. The tender you want
4. BOQ Comparative Chart

### If the file cannot be used

If BOQ+ cannot reliably use your file, it tells you so. In that case, the analysis uses BOQ+ market data instead. So you will know when a file has not been used.

## What My Track Record shows

My Track Record builds from the results you add. Set your firm name, and BOQ+ finds your bids in those results. For each tender, it shows wins, losses and margins.

The track record also shows your win rate and your typical margin versus L1 on tenders you lost. If you are not sure what L1 means, see [L1 Tender Meaning: What L1, L2 and L3 Mean in a Tender](/blog/what-is-l1-in-tender).

### When BOQ+ is not sure the bidder is you

Sometimes BOQ+ is unsure whether a bidder name in a result is your firm. In that case it asks 'is that your firm?' You can confirm the match or reject it. You know your own bids best, so check each name it asks about.

### Early numbers

With fewer than 5 tenders, the app warns that the numbers are early and should be treated with care. Keep that warning in mind when you read a margin from a small set of results.

## Sharing your chart with BOQ+ market data

Before each upload, BOQ+ asks 'Help improve BOQ+ market data?' You choose how the chart is used:

- To improve rates for everyone.
- Only for your own BidIQ.

Your BOQs, rates and track record stay private to your account.

## What you can do with this

A few practical steps help you get useful output from the app:

- Before you upload, check that the file is a BOQ Comparative Chart in .xls or .xlsx format and is no larger than 10 MB.
- Add results from tenders that are similar to the one you are pricing. A similar tender gives the BidIQ analysis more context to compare against.
- Set your firm name before you read your track record, so BOQ+ can find your bids in the results you added.
- Read your track record against the bids you remember making, and confirm or reject any 'is that your firm?' question as it applies to you.
- Look at the number of tenders behind each figure. With fewer than 5 tenders, treat the numbers as early.
- If you are new to the app, the [BOQ App Download: How to Install BOQ+ and Sign In](/blog/boq-plus-app-download-and-sign-in) guide covers setup. For rate checks, see [BOQ Price List in BOQ+: Checking Item Rates in the Prices Tab](/blog/check-item-rates-in-boq-plus-prices-tab).

Answers to common questions about the app are in the [BOQ+ FAQ](/faq).

## Things to keep in mind

- Screens and button names may change between app versions. If a label on your phone differs from this guide, use the label you see.
- Track-record numbers depend on which results you add. They are not a forecast of how you will do in future tenders.

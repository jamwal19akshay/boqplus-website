This article gives the CPWD DSR 2023 rate for ceramic glazed wall tiles, how that rate is built up, and the Kerala tender band for this item. The figures are from 25 November 2026.

## Ceramic tiles rate per sqm in CPWD DSR 2023: item 8.31

CPWD DSR 2023 item 8.31 covers ceramic glazed wall tiles. The rate is ₹1,267.95 per sqm. DSR 2023 rates are at Delhi market prices of April 2023.

The item covers Ist quality ceramic glazed wall tiles conforming to IS: 15622, with the thickness to be specified by the manufacturer. The tiles are for skirting, risers of steps and dados. They are fixed over a 12 mm thick bed of cement mortar 1:3 (1 cement : 3 coarse sand). Joints are made with grey cement slurry at 3.3 kg per sqm, and the item includes pointing in white cement mixed with pigment of matching shade.

The item covers all colours and shades except burgundy, bottle green and black. Tiles of any size are allowed, as approved by the Engineer-in-Charge.

## How the DSR rate for ceramic wall tiles is built up

CPWD's Delhi Analysis of Rates 2023 builds the ₹1,267.95 rate up from a base cost of ₹891.27 per sqm. The base cost covers labour, materials, sundries (including carriage) and other DSR items it is built from.

### Base cost and its shares

- Labour is 45.8% of the base cost, which is ₹408.25 per sqm.
- Materials are 27.8% of the base cost, which is ₹247.64 per sqm.
- Sundries (including carriage) are 18.7% of the base cost, which is ₹167.03 per sqm.
- Other DSR items the rate is built from are 7.7% of the base cost, which is ₹68.35 per sqm.

### The largest inputs

The largest single input is the ceramic glazed tile itself, at 25.9% of the base cost. The analysis puts the tile at ₹230.63 per sqm. The other main lines are:

- Mason 1st class (labour): ₹224.25 per sqm
- Coolie (labour): ₹184.00 per sqm
- Mortar for pointing in white cement (sundries): ₹91.78 per sqm
- Rate as per item 3.8, mortars (other DSR items): ₹68.35 per sqm
- Sundries including carriage of cement etc.: ₹61.09 per sqm

Part of this item is priced at the full rate of another DSR item. That item already includes its own profit and overheads.

### Add-ons on top of the base cost

1. CPWD adds 1% water charges: ₹8.91 per sqm.
2. GST is added using a multiplying factor of 0.2127: ₹191.47 per sqm.
3. CPWD adds 15% contractor's profit and overheads: ₹163.75 per sqm.
4. CPWD adds 1% cess: ₹12.55 per sqm.

Before the 15% profit and overheads and the 1% cess, CPWD's cost is ₹1,091.65 per sqm. That is 86.1% of the DSR rate.

## Pricing ceramic wall tiles for your own tender

The DSR rate is for Delhi at April 2023 prices. Your real cost for this item can be different. These things can move it:

- Location and lead: the distance from supplier to site can change the material and carriage lines.
- Site conditions: they can affect the labour and sundries lines.
- Price changes since April 2023: tile, cement and labour prices may have moved.
- Tile make and thickness: the manufacturer specifies the thickness, so get quotes for the exact product in your tender specification.

The tile, mason, coolie and mortar lines are the biggest lines in the build-up. Check these first against your own supplier and labour quotes.

If you are new to how a unit rate is built, [rate analysis of civil works](/blog/rate-analysis-civil-works-basics) explains the steps. You can also read how [CPWD DSR 2023](/blog/cpwd-dsr-2023-explained) is laid out, and see the [rate books and data sources](/sources) BOQ+ uses.

## When a quote falls below CPWD's cost before profit

CPWD's cost before profit and overheads is ₹1,091.65 per sqm. A quote below this figure is below CPWD's own cost build-up. It is not a guaranteed loss, because each contractor's costs differ.

Before you go below this level, check these points against your own records:

1. Your own quotes for the tile, with the thickness and make named in the specification.
2. Your mason and coolie rates for the work area, not the DSR labour rates.
3. The lead from the supplier and the carriage cost to site.
4. Wastage allowance for cutting and breakage.
5. The mortar and cement needed for the 12 mm bed and the grey slurry joints.

## Kerala tender results for this item

The BOQ+ database lists 72 tenders in Kerala for this exact DSR item with published results. These are tender-quoted rates, not market prices.

- Middle half of the tenders: the typical quoted rate, which is the median of each tender's bidders, ran from ₹1,138.43 to ₹1,300.00 per sqm. That is 10% below DSR to 3% above DSR 2023.
- Middle value across the tenders: ₹1,250.85 per sqm, which is 1% below DSR 2023.
- Lowest (L1) bids: the middle value was ₹1,191.30 per sqm, which is 6% below DSR 2023.
- In 18% of those tenders the typical quote was below ₹1,091.65, CPWD's cost before profit and overheads.

Compared with the other items we track in Kerala, this item sits close to DSR. Shuttering for columns, slabs and lintels, and the steel items, have typical quotes below DSR. Vitrified floor tiles 600x600, PCC, RCC, random rubble masonry and the excavation items have typical quotes above DSR. For the vitrified tile figures, see [vitrified tiles rate per sqm](/blog/vitrified-tiles-rate-per-sqm-dsr-2023).

## What the figures do not show

- These are rates quoted in published tender results. They are not market prices and not a recommended rate.
- The comparison is with the printed DSR 2023 rate (Delhi, April 2023 prices). Kerala estimates may add a cost index and local conditions, and older tenders may have used an earlier DSR.
- Each bidder's rate includes what they allowed for lead, site conditions and risk.

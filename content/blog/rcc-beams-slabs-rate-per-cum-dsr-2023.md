This article explains the CPWD DSR 2023 rate for RCC 1:1.5:3 in beams and slabs, how that rate is built up, and the band seen in Kerala tender results. The figures are as of 21 November 2026. The DSR rate itself uses Delhi market prices from April 2023.

## RCC 1:1.5:3 Rate per Cubic Meter in DSR 2023

CPWD DSR 2023 item 5.3 is ₹11,505.50 per cum. The item covers reinforced cement concrete work in beams, suspended floors, roofs having slope up to 15° landings, balconies, shelves, chajjas, lintels, bands, plain window sills, staircases and spiral stair cases above plinth level up to floor five level.

The rate uses a 1:1.5:3 mix: 1 part cement, 1.5 parts coarse sand (zone-III) derived from natural sources, and 3 parts graded stone aggregate of 20 mm nominal size derived from natural sources. DSR describes the item by this mix, not by a concrete grade name. If your BOQ names a grade, check it against this description before you use this rate.

The rate excludes the cost of centering, shuttering, finishing and reinforcement. Check how your BOQ lists these, because they are not part of this item's rate.

DSR 2023 rates are at Delhi market prices of April 2023.

## How the DSR Rate Is Built Up

CPWD's Delhi Analysis of Rates 2023 builds the ₹11,505.50 rate from a base cost of ₹8,087.45 per cum. The base cost covers labour, materials, machinery and sundries (including carriage).

In that analysis, the base cost splits as follows:

- Labour: 50.3% of the base cost (₹4,068.08 per cum)
- Materials: 48.0% of the base cost (₹3,882.90 per cum)
- Machinery: 1.3% of the base cost (₹104.00 per cum)
- Sundries (including carriage): 0.4% of the base cost (₹32.46 per cum)

The largest single input is Portland Cement (0.2833 cum), at ₹2,062.40 per cum, which is 25.5% of the base cost. Other main lines are:

- Beldar (labour): ₹2,024.00 per cum
- Coolie (labour): ₹1,104.00 per cum
- Stone aggregate, 20 mm nominal size (materials): ₹812.25 per cum
- Bhisti (labour): ₹734.40 per cum
- Coarse sand, zone III (materials): ₹616.25 per cum

On top of the base cost, CPWD adds:

- 1% water charges: ₹80.87 per cum
- GST, using a multiplying factor of 0.2127: ₹1,737.40 per cum
- 15% contractor's profit and overheads: ₹1,485.86 per cum
- 1% cess: ₹113.92 per cum

For background on how unit rates are put together, read [rate analysis of civil works](/blog/rate-analysis-civil-works-basics).

## Pricing It for Your Own Tender

The DSR is a Delhi price list at April 2023 prices. Your real cost depends on where the work is, how far materials travel and the conditions on your site. Prices may also have moved since then.

In general terms:

- Cement and aggregate prices, and their carriage, affect the materials and sundries lines.
- Local labour rates affect the labour lines, which are 50.3% of the base cost.
- Lead and site conditions can change your carriage and other site costs.
- Price changes since April 2023 can affect every line.

Use your own quotes for each input before you rely on any published figure. This rate excludes centering and shuttering, so also look at the [slab and roof shuttering rate](/blog/shuttering-rate-per-sqm-slabs-roofs-dsr-2023) and the [beam and lintel shuttering rate](/blog/shuttering-rate-per-sqm-lintels-beams-dsr-2023) when you price the same structure. To look up item rates while you fill a BOQ, see [checking item rates in the Prices tab](/blog/check-item-rates-in-boq-plus-prices-tab).

## When a Quote Falls Below CPWD's Cost

CPWD's cost before the 15% contractor's profit and overheads and the 1% cess is ₹9,905.72 per cum. That is 86.1% of the DSR rate. A quote below this is below CPWD's own cost build-up. It is not a guaranteed loss, because each contractor's real costs differ.

Before you go below this figure in any quote, check:

1. Your own cement, sand and aggregate quotes, including carriage to site.
2. Your labour rates for the trades you need.
3. Lead to the work location.
4. Wastage and any allowance for site conditions.
5. Any costs in your BOQ that this item does not include.

Then set your own cost before profit and overheads against the ₹9,905.72 figure, line by line, to see where your numbers differ from CPWD's.

## What Kerala Tenders Show

85 tenders in Kerala in our database list this exact DSR item with their published results. The figures below are tender-quoted rates, not market prices.

- In the middle half of those tenders, the typical quoted rate (the median of each tender's bidders) ran from ₹11,675.19 to ₹13,100.00 per cum. That is 1% above DSR to 14% above DSR 2023.
- The middle value across those tenders was ₹12,481.17 per cum, which is 8% above DSR 2023.
- The lowest (L1) bids for this item had a middle value of ₹11,815.24 per cum, which is 3% above DSR 2023.
- In 8% of those tenders, the typical quote was below ₹9,905.72, CPWD's cost before profit and overheads.

This item is usually quoted above DSR, but not every item is. RCC 1:1.5:3 up to plinth (DSR 5.1.2) is typically 8% above DSR 2023 across 170 tenders. PCC 1:4:8 (DSR 4.1.8) is typically 7% above across 204 tenders. Shuttering for slabs and roofs (DSR 5.9.3) is typically 6% below across 117 tenders, and steel reinforcement (TMT Fe-500D, DSR 5.22.6) is typically 4% below across 216 tenders.

## Limits of These Figures

- These are rates quoted in published tender results. They are not market prices and not a recommended rate.
- The comparison is with the printed DSR 2023 rate (Delhi, April 2023 prices). Kerala estimates may add a cost index and local conditions, and older tenders may have used an earlier DSR.
- Each bidder's rate includes what they allowed for lead, site conditions and risk.

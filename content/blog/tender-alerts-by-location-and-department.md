This guide shows how to find tenders by location and department in the BOQ+ tender app, and how to set alerts for new matches. The steps and screens are as of 14 November 2026.

## Tender App Search: Find Tenders by State and Department

The Tenders tab lists tenders collected from public e-procurement portals. The list is updated daily. You can search tenders by work, department or place, and you can narrow the list with filter chips.

### Filter chips

The chips let you narrow tenders by:

- State
- Work type
- 'Closing ≤ 7 days'
- 'My departments'
- Estimated value
- A 'More' sheet

### The 'More' sheet

The 'More' sheet adds portal, organisation, closing date, a location search such as a city or district, and an EMD amount range. Use the location search to look for a particular city or district.

### Closing soon

A 'Closing soon' strip shows tenders closing this week. Together with the 'Closing ≤ 7 days' chip, it shows you which tenders have a deadline coming up.

## Set Up Your Tender Alerts

The first time you open the Tenders tab, BOQ+ asks 'What tenders should we show you?' You choose:

1. Work type: Electrical, Civil, Roads or Plumbing.
2. Your state. The choices are J&K, HP, Punjab, Haryana, UP, Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Central, or All India.
3. Optionally, up to 5 organisations.

You can skip this step and change it later. To change your alert choices, tap the bell icon (Alert settings) at the top of the Tenders tab.

BOQ+ can send a push notification when new tenders match the work type, state or organisations you saved. Alerts are not sent by WhatsApp. Push alerts need notification permission on your phone, so check that it is switched on.

## Save, Open and Upload a BOQ

Each tender can be saved with a bookmark, shared, or opened on the portal. The detail view also says 'Got the BOQ? Upload to price it'. The steps are:

1. Open the tender on the portal.
2. Download the BOQ from the portal.
3. Upload the BOQ in BOQ+.

For the pricing steps after the upload, see [How to Price a BOQ in BOQ+](/blog/price-e-tender-boq-file-in-boq-plus).

### Deadline reminders

You can set a deadline reminder for a BOQ you upload. On Android, you pick a lead time of 1 day, 3 days or 1 week before. The reminder is kept on your device. On iPhone, the tender reminder is 1 day before.

## What You Can Do With This

- Set your work type and state first, so the list matches the kind of work you do.
- Add the organisations you deal with most often, up to 5.
- Use the 'Closing ≤ 7 days' chip and the 'Closing soon' strip to see what is closing this week.
- Use the location search in 'More' to look at a city or district.
- Bookmark tenders you want to come back to.
- Download the BOQ from the portal, upload it in BOQ+ and set a deadline reminder.

If you have not installed the app yet, the [BOQ App Download guide](/blog/boq-plus-app-download-and-sign-in) explains the install and sign-in steps.

## Things to Check

- Screens and button names may change between app versions.
- The state list in the app is shorter than the list of portals BOQ+ reads. The [Data sources page](/sources) shows the portals.
- Deadline reminder lead times differ by phone. On iPhone, the tender reminder is 1 day before.
- Push alerts need notification permission on your phone.

This guide shows how to upload a tender BOQ file to BOQ+ and get it ready for pricing. The steps follow the 3.0.0 apps and reflect the screens as of 6 November 2026.

## Price BOQ files in the app, one step at a time

BOQ+ handles both item-rate BOQs, where you enter a rate for each line, and percentage tenders, where you set one Bid % above or below the department's estimate.

### Before you start

- Have the BOQ file ready. BOQ+ reads Excel files (.xls and .xlsx, up to 5 MB) and digital PDFs (up to 10 MB).
- Be signed in. Uploading a BOQ needs a signed-in account.
- Know how you will start pricing. Opening a BOQ is a free preview. Starting to price needs a Quarterly or Annual plan, or 1 purchased BOQ credit.

### Upload the file

1. Open the BOQs tab and tap the round + button. The phone's file picker opens straight away.
2. Choose the BOQ file. A GePNIC .xls downloaded from the tender page can be uploaded as it is.
3. If the Excel file has more than one sheet, BOQ+ asks which sheet holds the BOQ. Pick the sheet with the line items.
4. If you are using the app as a guest, BOQ+ asks you to sign in first. It keeps your chosen file while you do.

On Android, you can also open a BOQ file with BOQ+ from another app, or share it to BOQ+ from apps such as WhatsApp or Google Drive.

### Check the BOQ Parsed summary

After reading the file, BOQ+ shows a 'BOQ Parsed' summary. It shows the BOQ name, how many line items it found and how confident the reading is. If confidence is low, BOQ+ asks you to review item descriptions and quantities before pricing.

Do this check before you enter any rates. Read the item descriptions and quantities against your original tender file.

### Start pricing

To start pricing, tap Start Pricing. This is included with a Quarterly or Annual plan, or it uses 1 purchased BOQ credit.

The free plan covers previews. Starting to price needs a plan or a purchased credit, so check which one you have before you tap.

### Fill in the rates

Once you start pricing, you can:

- Fill rates line by line.
- Run Auto-fill from a rate book.
- Use the search box to find an item in a long BOQ.

To see which rate books and data sources sit behind the app, read the [rate books and data sources BOQ+ uses](/sources) page.

## What to do with your priced BOQ

Work through the BOQ line by line. If you use Auto-fill, check each value against your own material quotes, labour rates and site conditions before you rely on it.

If the tender is a percentage tender, you set one Bid % above or below the department's estimate. For how the two tender types differ, read [the difference between item rate and percentage rate tenders](/blog/item-rate-vs-percentage-rate-tender).

Keep the BOQ+ figures separate from your final bid. BOQ+ does not submit your bid. You still log in to the official portal with your DSC and enter your final figures there. Compare the portal entries with your priced BOQ before you submit.

For the form itself, see [how to fill a BOQ for an e-tender](/blog/how-to-fill-boq-government-e-tender). If you have questions about the app, the [BOQ+ FAQ](/faq) is a good place to start.

## Points to keep in mind

- Screens and button names may change between app versions.
- This guide follows the 3.0.0 apps. Android and iPhone differ in small details, which are noted where they matter.
- Free previews and starting to price are different steps. The free plan covers previews. Starting to price needs a plan or a purchased credit.

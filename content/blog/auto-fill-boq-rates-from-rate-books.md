This guide explains how Auto-fill in BOQ+ matches the items in your bill of quantities against built-in rate books and fills the rates it is confident about. It describes the app as of 11 November 2026, for the 3.0.0 apps.

## BOQ software that fills rates from rate books

BOQ+ is a mobile app for pricing tender BOQs. Its Auto-fill tool can fill empty rate lines in a BOQ from a rate book stored inside the app. You start it from the Auto-fill chip on a BOQ you have started pricing.

The rate books are stored inside the app, and the matching runs on the phone without using the internet.

### Rate books built into the app

The central rate books are:

- CPWD DSR 2023 Civil Works (Vol 1 and Vol 2)
- CPWD DSR E&M 2025
- CPWD DSR E&M 2014
- MES SSR 2020 (Military Engineer Services)
- Indian Railways USSOR 2019

The J&K rate book is the JK PWD SOR 2022 (J&K Schedule of Rates 2022).

The state rate books are:

- HP PWD SOR 2020 (Himachal Pradesh)
- Maharashtra PWD SSR 2022-23
- Maharashtra PWD SSR Electrical
- Karnataka Common Schedule of Rates 2023-24 (Vol 2, Buildings)
- Telangana Common SoR 2022-23

For the current list of rate books, see [Rate books and data sources BOQ+ uses](/sources). For a plain explanation of the CPWD schedule, see [CPWD DSR 2023 Explained](/blog/cpwd-dsr-2023-explained).

### How to run Auto-fill

1. Open the BOQ you have started pricing in BOQ+.
2. Tap the Auto-fill chip.
3. Choose a rate book to match against.
4. Wait for Auto-fill to finish.
5. Read the summary and the list of items that still need rates.

When Auto-fill finishes, it shows how many items were filled from the chosen book. It also lists the items that still need rates.

You can then pick another rate book. It only fills the empty lines. Rates already filled are never overwritten.

Weaker matches appear as suggestions only. They are not written into your BOQ. Each filled rate shows its source book and item code in the expanded row, so you can see where it came from.

### Matching against your own department's SSR

You can also upload your own department's SSR as a PDF and match against it. Digital PDFs work best. Scanned PDFs may match less well.

### Browsing and comparing books

The Rates sub-tab lets you browse and search every built-in book. You can also compare two books side by side.

## What a contractor can do with this

Auto-fill gives you a first pass on the empty lines. It does not replace your own estimate. A practical way to use it:

- Run Auto-fill on the BOQ and note how many items were filled and which items remain.
- Open each filled row and check the source book and item code. Confirm the item description matches the work in the tender.
- Price the remaining items yourself, using your own material quotes, labour rates and site conditions.
- Check the filled rates against your own costs before you use them in a bid.
- Use the Rates sub-tab to browse a book or compare two books side by side when you want a second look at an item.

## Things to keep in mind

### Limits of Auto-fill

Auto-fill is a starting point. It fills only what it matches, so some lines will stay empty. You should check every rate before you bid.

Screen names and button names may change between app versions. This guide covers the 3.0.0 apps. Rate books are added over time, so the [data sources page](/sources) lists the current ones.

### Android and iPhone differ

Android groups CPWD DSR 2023 Vol 1 and Vol 2 as one book. The iPhone app lists them separately.

This article explains the CPWD DSR 2023 rate for earthwork excavation in foundation trenches, how that rate is built up, and the band seen in Kerala tender results. The tender figures are as of 5 November 2026.

## Earthwork excavation rate per cum in foundation trenches

### What the item covers

DSR 2023 item 2.8.1 covers earth work in excavation in foundation trenches or drains, by mechanical means (hydraulic excavator) or manual means. The trench must not exceed 1.5 m in width or 10 sqm on plan. The rate includes dressing of sides, ramming of bottoms, all lifts, getting out the excavated soil and disposal of surplus soil as directed, within a lead of 50 m. It applies to all kinds of soil.

### The DSR 2023 rate

The CPWD DSR 2023 rate for item 2.8.1 is ₹260.30 per cum. DSR 2023 rates are at Delhi market prices of April 2023, as stated in the DSR 2023 preface.

## How the ₹260.30 rate is built up

### Base cost and its shares

CPWD Delhi Analysis of Rates 2023 builds the rate up from a base cost of ₹182.97 per cum. The base cost covers machinery, labour and sundries (including carriage). Its shares are:

- Machinery: 95.4% of base cost, ₹174.59 per cum
- Labour: 4.2% of base cost, ₹7.68 per cum
- Sundries (including carriage): 0.4% of base cost, ₹0.70 per cum

### The largest inputs

The largest single input is Hydraulic Excavator (3D) with driver and fuel, at ₹94.19 per cum, or 51.5% of base cost. Other analysis lines include:

- Hire and running charges of loader (machinery): ₹80.39 per cum
- Coolie (labour): ₹4.42 per cum
- Mate (labour): ₹3.26 per cum
- Sundries, such as watch and ward and minor T&P: ₹0.70 per cum

### Add-ons to reach the DSR rate

On top of the base cost, CPWD adds:

- 1% water charges: ₹1.83 per cum
- GST, using a multiplying factor of 0.2127: ₹39.31 per cum
- 15% contractor's profit and overheads: ₹33.62 per cum
- 1% cess: ₹2.58 per cum

## Pricing it for your own tender

The DSR is a Delhi price list from April 2023. Your real cost on a Kerala site depends on your location, lead and carriage, site conditions and any price changes since then. These factors can change the machinery, labour and carriage parts of the build-up. Machinery is the largest share of the base cost, so machine hire and fuel are a good place to start.

A simple checklist before you price this item:

1. Get current hire rates for excavators and loaders in your area.
2. Check local rates for coolies and mates.
3. Compare the lead to your disposal site with the 50 m in the DSR description.
4. Note site conditions such as soil, water and access.
5. Note how much dressing and ramming your drawings and the specification call for.

For how item rates differ from percentage rates in a BOQ, see [item rate vs percentage rate tender](/blog/item-rate-vs-percentage-rate-tender).

## When a quote goes below CPWD's own cost

CPWD's cost before the 15% contractor's profit and overheads and the 1% cess is ₹224.11 per cum. That is 86.1% of the DSR rate. This is CPWD's own cost build-up, not a guaranteed loss point. Each contractor's real costs differ.

A quote below ₹224.11 per cum is below CPWD's own cost build-up. Before going below that level, check your own figures:

- Your machine hire and fuel quotes
- Your labour rates and crew size
- The lead and carriage you will actually need
- Expected wastage and the soil you will remove

Add up your own cost for each of these per cum, then compare the total with ₹224.11. The result shows how your costs compare with CPWD's, and nothing more.

## What Kerala tender results show

Our database lists 187 Kerala tenders with published results for this exact DSR item. These are tender-quoted rates, not market prices. The typical quote for each tender is the median of its bidders.

- In the middle half of those tenders, the typical quote ran from ₹310.00 to ₹355.98 per cum. That is 19% above to 37% above DSR 2023.
- The middle value across those tenders was ₹327.87 per cum, which is 26% above DSR 2023.
- The lowest (L1) bids had a middle value of ₹311.27 per cum, which is 20% above DSR 2023. To learn what L1 means, see [L1 tender meaning](/blog/what-is-l1-in-tender).
- None of those tenders had a typical quote below ₹224.11 per cum, CPWD's cost before profit and overheads.

This item is typically quoted above DSR 2023 in Kerala. Other items we track vary. Shuttering for columns and pillars (DSR 5.9.6) is typically 6% below DSR 2023 across 85 tenders, and steel reinforcement TMT Fe-500D (DSR 5.22.6) is typically 4% below across 216 tenders. PCC 1:4:8 (DSR 4.1.8) is typically 7% above across 204 tenders, and earthwork excavation over areas (DSR 2.6.1) is typically 37% above across 141 tenders.

## Caveats

- These are rates quoted in published tender results, not market prices and not a recommended rate.
- The comparison is with the printed DSR 2023 rate (Delhi, April 2023 prices). Kerala estimates may add a cost index and local conditions, and older tenders may have used an earlier DSR.
- Each bidder's rate includes what they allowed for lead, site conditions and risk.

This article gives the CPWD DSR 2023 rate for shuttering for slabs and roofs, how that rate is built up, and where quoted rates in Kerala tenders have fallen. The figures are as of 19 November 2026.

## Shuttering rate per sqm in the DSR 2023

### The DSR 2023 rate

CPWD DSR 2023 item 5.9.3 (Shuttering for slabs and roofs) is ₹927.25 per sqm. The item covers centering and shuttering including strutting, propping etc. and removal of form for suspended floors, roofs, landings, balconies and access platform.

DSR 2023 rates are at Delhi market prices of April 2023, as stated in the DSR 2023 preface. For a wider look at what the book holds, see [CPWD DSR 2023 explained](/blog/cpwd-dsr-2023-explained).

### How the rate is built up

CPWD's Delhi Analysis of Rates 2023 builds the ₹927.25 rate from a base cost of ₹675.55 per sqm. The base cost is split as follows:

- Labour: 77.9% of the base cost (₹526.44 per sqm)
- Other DSR items it is built from: 11.8% (₹80.02 per sqm)
- Sundries, including carriage: 7.0% (₹47.05 per sqm)
- Materials: 3.3% (₹22.05 per sqm)

The largest single input is Beldar at 48.4% of the base cost, which is ₹327.11 per sqm. Fitter (grade 1) labour is ₹199.33 per sqm. The other DSR item is the rate as per item no.10.1 S.H. steel work, at ₹80.02 per sqm. This part is priced at the full rate of that item, which already includes its own profit and overheads.

Other lines are carriage at ₹21.86 per sqm, shuttering oil at ₹13.12 per sqm and adjustable span ESO+SI (2.35-3.40) at ₹12.13 per sqm.

On top of the base cost, CPWD adds 1% water charges (₹5.96), GST using a multiplying factor of 0.2127 (₹127.94), 15% contractor's profit and overheads (₹109.41) and 1% cess (₹8.39), all per sqm. [Rate analysis of civil works](/blog/rate-analysis-civil-works-basics) explains what each part of a unit rate means.

## Pricing this item for your own tender

### What moves your real cost

The DSR is Delhi at April 2023 prices. Your real cost on a Kerala job can be different. Location, lead and carriage, site conditions and price changes since April 2023 all move it. In general terms, these parts of the build-up are affected:

- Labour, which is the largest share of the base cost. Local rates for beldar and fitters change the build-up the most.
- Carriage and other sundries, such as shuttering oil, which change with distance and site access.
- Materials such as the adjustable span, which change with local supply prices.
- The steel work part, which depends on the build-up of that other DSR item.

### When a quote goes below CPWD's cost

Before the 15% contractor's profit and overheads and the 1% cess, CPWD's cost is ₹809.45 per sqm. That is 87.3% of the DSR rate. This is CPWD's own cost build-up, not a guaranteed loss point: each contractor's real costs differ.

So a quote below ₹809.45 per sqm is below CPWD's own cost build-up before profit and overheads. Before you go below that figure on your own estimate, check:

1. Your own quotes for shuttering material and hire.
2. Your labour rates for beldar and fitters on the site.
3. Lead and carriage from your store to the site.
4. Wastage.

## Kerala tender results for this item

117 tenders in Kerala in our database list this exact DSR item with their published results. These are tender-quoted rates, not market prices.

In the middle half of those tenders, the typical quoted rate (the median of each tender's bidders) ran from ₹795.77 to ₹931.50 per sqm. That is 14% below DSR to about the same as DSR 2023. The middle value across those tenders was ₹875.50 per sqm, which is 6% below DSR 2023. The lowest (L1) bids had a middle value of ₹846.13 per sqm, which is 9% below DSR 2023.

In 26% of those tenders the typical quote was below ₹809.45, CPWD's cost before profit and overheads.

### How this item compares with other items

These are the typical Kerala quotes for some other items in our database:

- Shuttering for columns and pillars (DSR 5.9.6): 6% below DSR 2023, across 85 tenders
- Shuttering for lintels and beams (DSR 5.9.5): 5% below DSR 2023, across 133 tenders
- Steel reinforcement (TMT Fe-500D) (DSR 5.22.6): 4% below DSR 2023, across 216 tenders
- Vitrified floor tiles 600x600 (DSR 11.41.2): 4% above DSR 2023, across 80 tenders
- RCC 1:1.5:3 beams and slabs (DSR 5.3): 8% above DSR 2023, across 85 tenders
- Earthwork excavation over areas (DSR 2.6.1): 37% above DSR 2023, across 141 tenders

Like shuttering for columns and pillars, and for lintels and beams, the typical quote for this item is below DSR 2023. Several other items, such as RCC, vitrified tiles and earthwork, have a typical quote above DSR 2023. ## Caveats on these figures

- These are rates quoted in published tender results, not market prices and not a recommended rate.
- The comparison is with the printed DSR 2023 rate (Delhi, April 2023 prices). Kerala estimates may add a cost index and local conditions, and older tenders may have used an earlier DSR.
- Each bidder's rate includes what they allowed for lead, site conditions and risk.

This article explains the CPWD DSR 2023 rate for earthwork excavation over areas, item 2.6.1. It covers how that rate is built up and what Kerala tender results show for this item. The figures are as of 17 November 2026.

## Excavation rate analysis for earthwork over areas

### What DSR 2023 item 2.6.1 covers

CPWD DSR 2023 item 2.6.1 (Earthwork excavation over areas) is ₹177.50 per cum. The item is described as earth work in excavation by mechanical means (hydraulic excavator) or manual means over areas exceeding 30 cm in depth, 1.5 m in width and 10 sqm on plan.

The item includes getting out and disposal of the excavated earth, with lead up to 50 m, and all lift, as directed by the Engineer-in-charge. It applies to all kinds of soil.

### How the rate is built up

The CPWD Delhi Analysis of Rates 2023 builds the ₹177.50 rate up from a base cost of ₹124.77 per cum. The base cost covers machinery, labour and sundries (including carriage). Its shares are:

- Machinery: 93.3% of the base cost, which is ₹116.38 per cum.
- Labour: 6.2% of the base cost, which is ₹7.68 per cum.
- Sundries (including carriage): 0.6% of the base cost, which is ₹0.70 per cum.

The largest single input is the hydraulic excavator (3D) with driver and fuel, at 50.3% of the base cost. Its analysis line is ₹62.79 per cum. Other lines in the analysis include:

- Hire and running charges of loader (machinery): ₹53.59 per cum.
- Coolie (labour): ₹4.42 per cum.
- Mate (labour): ₹3.26 per cum.
- Sundries, such as watch and ward and minor T&P: ₹0.70 per cum.

On top of the base cost, CPWD adds:

1. 1% water charges: ₹1.25 per cum.
2. GST, using a multiplying factor of 0.2127: ₹26.80 per cum.
3. 15% contractor's profit and overheads: ₹22.92 per cum.
4. 1% cess: ₹1.76 per cum.

For more on how a unit rate is put together, see [rate analysis of civil works](/blog/rate-analysis-civil-works-basics).

## Pricing it for your own tender

DSR 2023 rates are at Delhi market prices of April 2023. Your location, lead and carriage, site conditions and price changes since then can all move your real cost.

Machinery makes up most of the base cost. Hire and running rates for excavators and loaders in your area, and fuel, are therefore the first figures to check against your own quotes. Labour rates for coolies and mates also feed into the build-up. Your lead and carriage and your site conditions can make your machinery, labour and carriage costs differ from the Delhi build-up.

A practical way to use this:

1. Match your BOQ item to DSR 2.6.1 on depth, width, plan area, soil type, lead and lift.
2. Build your own cost for machinery, labour and carriage at your site.
3. Compare that cost with the DSR 2023 rate of ₹177.50 per cum and with CPWD's cost figure below.
4. Use the Kerala tender band as context only, not as a target.

To see how CPWD rates are adjusted for later price changes, read [CPWD cost index explained](/blog/cpwd-cost-index-explained).

## Where a quote stops covering CPWD's cost

Before the 15% contractor's profit and overheads and the 1% cess, CPWD's cost is ₹152.82 per cum. That is 86.1% of the DSR rate. This is CPWD's own cost build-up, not a guaranteed loss point. Each contractor's real costs differ.

A quote below ₹152.82 per cum is below CPWD's own cost build-up. Before going below it, check:

- Your own machinery hire and fuel quotes for excavators and loaders.
- Your labour rates for coolies and mates.
- The lead and carriage your site needs.
- The wastage you expect on the excavated earth.

To do the arithmetic, add up your own costs for each of these, then compare your total per cum with CPWD's figure and with the rate you are considering.

## What Kerala tender results show

Our database lists 141 tenders in Kerala with published results for this exact DSR item. These are tender-quoted rates, not market prices.

In the middle half of those tenders, the typical quoted rate (the median of each tender's bidders) ran from ₹224.00 to ₹257.63 per cum. That is 26% above DSR to 45% above DSR 2023. The middle value across those tenders was ₹243.20 per cum, which is 37% above DSR 2023.

The lowest (L1) bids for this item had a middle value of ₹236.63 per cum, which is 33% above DSR 2023. None of those tenders had a typical quote below ₹152.82, CPWD's cost before profit and overheads.

This item is usually quoted above DSR in Kerala. Other items in our list vary. Typical Kerala quotes for shuttering for columns and pillars (DSR 5.9.6) are 6% below DSR 2023, across 85 tenders. PCC 1:2:4 (DSR 4.1.3) is 10% above, across 164 tenders. Earthwork excavation in foundation trenches (DSR 2.8.1) is 26% above DSR 2023, across 187 tenders. Excavation over areas, at 37% above for the middle value, sits above these three. You can find more tender data on the [BOQ+ blog](/blog).

## Caveats to keep in mind

- These are rates quoted in published tender results. They are not market prices and not a recommended rate.
- The comparison is with the printed DSR 2023 rate, which is Delhi at April 2023 prices. Kerala estimates may add a cost index and local conditions. Older tenders may have used an earlier DSR.
- Each bidder's rate includes what they allowed for lead, site conditions and risk.

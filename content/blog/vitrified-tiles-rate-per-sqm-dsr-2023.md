This article gives the CPWD DSR 2023 rate for 600x600 vitrified floor tiles, how that rate is built up, and the band of rates quoted in Kerala tenders. The figures are as of 15 November 2026.

## Vitrified tiles rate per sqm in CPWD DSR 2023

### The DSR 2023 rate for item 11.41.2

CPWD DSR 2023 item 11.41.2 is vitrified floor tiles 600x600. The rate is ₹1,553.45 per sqm.

DSR 2023 describes the item as providing and laying vitrified floor tiles in different sizes, with water absorption less than 0.08% and conforming to IS: 15622, of approved make, in all colours and shades. The tiles are laid on 20 mm thick cement mortar 1:4 and jointed with grey cement slurry at 3.3 kg per sqm. The joints are grouted with white cement and matching pigments. The thickness is to be specified by the manufacturer.

### How the rate is built up

CPWD's Delhi Analysis of Rates 2023 builds the ₹1,553.45 rate from a base cost of ₹1,091.95 per sqm. The base cost splits into four shares:

- Materials: 53.2% of base cost (₹580.76 per sqm)
- Labour: 29.9% of base cost (₹326.60 per sqm)
- Other DSR items it is built from: 9.3% of base cost (₹101.08 per sqm)
- Sundries, including carriage: 7.6% of base cost (₹83.51 per sqm)

The largest single input is vitrified floor tile 60x60 cm, at 51.6% of base cost. The analysis puts this tile at ₹563.75 per sqm. Other lines include Mason 1st class at ₹179.40 per sqm, Coolie at ₹147.20 per sqm, and Portland Cement at ₹17.01 per sqm. The line for sundries including carriage of cement etc. is ₹61.09 per sqm.

Part of this item is priced at the full rate of another DSR item, which already includes its own profit and overheads. For the mortar line, the analysis uses the rate of Item Number 3.9 of SH: Mortars, at ₹101.08 per sqm.

On top of the base cost, CPWD adds these per sqm amounts:

- 1% water charges: ₹10.92
- GST, using a multiplying factor of 0.2127: ₹234.58
- 15% contractor's profit and overheads: ₹200.62
- 1% cess: ₹15.38

Before the 15% contractor's profit and overheads and the 1% cess, CPWD's cost is ₹1,337.45 per sqm. That is 86.1% of the DSR rate. For a fuller explanation of how these parts fit together, see [Rate Analysis of Civil Works: What Goes Into a Unit Rate](/blog/rate-analysis-civil-works-basics).

## Pricing vitrified tiles for your own tender

### What moves your real cost

DSR 2023 rates are at Delhi market prices of April 2023. Your own location, lead and carriage, site conditions, and price changes since then can all move your real cost.

In general terms, these parts of the build-up are the ones most likely to move:

- The tile line, which is the largest input, so your own tile quotes matter most.
- Labour, through the mason and coolie lines.
- Carriage and sundries, which depend on how far materials travel to site.
- Mortar, which the analysis prices at the full rate of another DSR item. Your own mortar cost may differ from that rate.

For more on what else the schedule holds, see [CPWD DSR 2023 Explained](/blog/cpwd-dsr-2023-explained).

### When a quote goes below CPWD's cost

CPWD's cost before profit and overheads and the 1% cess is ₹1,337.45 per sqm. A quote below this figure is below CPWD's own cost build-up. That is not a guaranteed loss point, because each contractor's costs differ.

Before you go below this figure, check these points against your own records:

- Your own quotes for the 60x60 cm tile and for cement
- Your labour rates for masons and coolies
- Your lead and carriage to site
- Expected wastage on cutting and laying

The arithmetic is simple. Build up your own cost per sqm line by line, then compare the total with ₹1,337.45. The result shows where your cost sits against CPWD's own build-up.

## What Kerala tender results show

### The tender-quoted band

Our database lists 80 tenders in Kerala for this exact DSR item, with their published results. These are tender-quoted rates, not market prices.

In the middle half of those tenders, the typical quoted rate (the median of each tender's bidders) ran from ₹1,530.39 to ₹1,714.22 per sqm. That is 1% below DSR to 10% above DSR 2023. The middle value across the tenders was ₹1,616.19 per sqm, which is 4% above DSR 2023.

For the lowest (L1) bids, the middle value was ₹1,563.20 per sqm, which is 1% above DSR 2023. In 14% of these tenders, the typical quote was below ₹1,337.45, CPWD's cost before profit and overheads.

### How this item compares with other items

These are the typical Kerala quotes for some other DSR items, against DSR 2023:

- Shuttering for columns and pillars: 6% below, across 85 tenders
- Shuttering for lintels and beams: 5% below, across 133 tenders
- Ceramic glazed wall tiles: 1% below, across 72 tenders
- PCC 1:4:8: 7% above, across 204 tenders
- RCC 1:1.5:3 beams and slabs: 8% above, across 85 tenders
- Earthwork excavation over areas: 37% above, across 141 tenders

The middle value for vitrified floor tiles is above DSR 2023, as it is for the concrete and earthwork items listed. The shuttering and ceramic wall tile items have typical quotes below DSR 2023. For more on reading tender results, see [How to Read a Comparative Statement in a Tender](/blog/how-to-read-tender-comparative-statement).

## Caveats to keep in mind

- These are rates quoted in published tender results. They are not market prices and not a recommended rate.
- The comparison is with the printed DSR 2023 rate, which is Delhi at April 2023 prices. Kerala estimates may add a cost index and local conditions. Older tenders may have used an earlier DSR.
- Each bidder's rate includes what they allowed for lead, site conditions and risk.

This article gives the CPWD DSR 2023 rate for shuttering lintels and beams, shows how that rate is built up, and compares it with what Kerala tenders quoted. The figures are as of 9 November 2026.

## Centering and Shuttering Rate per Sqm in DSR 2023

CPWD DSR 2023 item 5.9.5 is shuttering for lintels and beams. The rate is ₹736.40 per sqm.

The DSR describes the item as centering and shuttering including strutting, propping etc. and removal of form for lintels, beams, plinth beams, girders, bressumers and cantilevers.

DSR 2023 rates are at Delhi market prices of April 2023, as stated in the DSR 2023 preface.

## How the DSR Rate Is Built Up

CPWD's Delhi Analysis of Rates 2023 builds the ₹736.40 rate from a base cost of ₹546.39 per sqm. The base cost covers labour, other DSR items it is built from, sundries (including carriage) and materials.

### Base cost and its shares

- Labour: 69.5% of the base cost (₹379.64 per sqm)
- Other DSR items it is built from: 17.7% (₹96.82 per sqm)
- Sundries (including carriage): 8.7% (₹47.64 per sqm)
- Materials: 4.1% (₹22.29 per sqm)

Beldar is the largest single input at 43.2% of the base cost, or ₹235.89 per sqm. Fitter (grade 1) is ₹143.75 per sqm.

Other lines in the analysis are:

- Rate as per item no.10.1 S.H. steel work, ₹96.82 per sqm
- Carriage, ₹22.70 per sqm
- Adjustable telescopic prop 3 m (2.02-3.75 m), ₹17.18 per sqm
- Shuttering oil, ₹11.35 per sqm

Part of this item is priced at the full rate of another DSR item. That item already includes its own profit and overheads.

### Add-ons on top of the base cost

CPWD then adds 1% water charges (₹4.50), GST using a multiplying factor of 0.2127 (₹96.58), 15% contractor's profit and overheads (₹82.60) and 1% cess (₹6.33), all per sqm.

## Pricing It for Your Own Tender

The DSR is Delhi at April 2023 prices. Your location, lead and carriage, site conditions and price changes since then can all move your real cost. In general terms:

1. Labour lines such as beldar and fitter depend on the wage rates where you work.
2. Carriage and sundries such as shuttering oil depend on your lead to site and your supplier quotes.
3. Materials such as telescopic props depend on current supplier quotes.
4. The steel work line is priced at the full rate of another DSR item, so changes in that item's cost carry into this one.

Check the item description in your tender against DSR 5.9.5 before you price it. Make sure the member types match the list above.

## Quoting Below CPWD's Cost Build-Up

Before the 15% contractor's profit and overheads and the 1% cess, CPWD's cost is ₹647.47 per sqm. That is 87.9% of the DSR rate.

A quote below ₹647.47 is below CPWD's own cost build-up. This is not a guaranteed loss point, because each contractor's real costs differ.

Before you go below that figure, check your own material quotes, labour rates, lead and wastage. Work out the arithmetic with your own numbers.

## Kerala Tender Results for This Item

133 tenders in Kerala in our database list this exact DSR item with their published results. These are tender-quoted rates, not market prices.

- Middle half of the tenders, using each tender's median bidder rate: ₹660.00 to ₹752.01 per sqm. That is 10% below DSR to 2% above DSR 2023.
- Middle value across the tenders: ₹696.04 per sqm, which is 5% below DSR 2023.
- Middle value of the lowest (L1) bids: ₹675.00 per sqm, which is 8% below DSR 2023.
- In 22% of those tenders the typical quote was below ₹647.47, CPWD's cost before profit and overheads.

For comparison, these are other items in the same results:

- Shuttering for columns and pillars (DSR 5.9.6): typical Kerala quote 6% below DSR 2023, across 85 tenders.
- Shuttering for slabs and roofs (DSR 5.9.3): typical Kerala quote 6% below DSR 2023, across 117 tenders.
- Steel reinforcement (TMT Fe-500D): typical Kerala quote 4% below DSR 2023, across 216 tenders.
- PCC 1:2:4: typical Kerala quote 10% above DSR 2023, across 164 tenders.
- RCC 1:1.5:3 beams and slabs: typical Kerala quote 8% above DSR 2023, across 85 tenders.
- Earthwork excavation in foundation trenches: typical Kerala quote 26% above DSR 2023, across 187 tenders.

So lintels and beams are usually quoted below DSR, as are the two other shuttering items and steel reinforcement. The PCC, RCC and earthwork items listed are usually quoted above it. For the earthwork item, see the [earthwork excavation rate article](/blog/earthwork-excavation-rate-per-cum-dsr-2023).

## Caveats to Keep in Mind

- These are rates quoted in published tender results, not market prices and not a recommended rate.
- The comparison is with the printed DSR 2023 rate (Delhi, April 2023 prices). Kerala estimates may add a cost index and local conditions, and older tenders may have used an earlier DSR.
- Each bidder's rate includes what they allowed for lead, site conditions and risk.

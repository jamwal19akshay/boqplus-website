This article gives the CPWD DSR 2023 rate for RCC 1:1.5:3 up to plinth level, shows how that rate is built up, and sets out the band of rates quoted in Kerala tenders. The figures are as of 11 November 2026.

## RCC rate per cubic meter in CPWD DSR 2023 (up to plinth)

DSR 2023 item 5.1.2 covers providing and laying a specified grade of reinforced cement concrete in position. The item excludes centering, shuttering, finishing and reinforcement. It covers all work up to plinth level, in the mix 1:1.5:3. That means 1 part cement, 1.5 parts coarse sand (zone-III) from natural sources, and 3 parts graded stone aggregate of 20 mm nominal size from natural sources.

The printed CPWD DSR 2023 rate is ₹9,045.75 per cum (cubic metre). DSR 2023 rates are at Delhi market prices of April 2023.

## How the rate is built up

CPWD's Delhi Analysis of Rates 2023 builds the ₹9,045.75 rate up from a base cost of ₹6,358.44 per cum. The base cost covers materials, labour, machinery and sundries, including carriage. Its shares are:

- Materials: 61.1% of base cost, ₹3,882.90 per cum
- Labour: 37.0% of base cost, ₹2,352.09 per cum
- Machinery: 1.4% of base cost, ₹91.00 per cum
- Sundries (including carriage): 0.5% of base cost, ₹32.46 per cum

The largest single input is Portland Cement, at 0.2833 cum and ₹2,062.40 per cum. That is 32.4% of the base cost. Other lines in the analysis include:

- Beldar (labour): ₹1,472.00 per cum
- Stone aggregate, 20 mm nominal size (materials): ₹812.25 per cum
- Bhisti (labour): ₹734.40 per cum
- Coarse sand, zone III (materials): ₹616.25 per cum
- Stone aggregate, 10 mm nominal size (materials): ₹392.00 per cum

On top of the base cost, CPWD adds:

- 1% water charges: ₹63.58 per cum
- GST, using a multiplying factor of 0.2127: ₹1,365.97 per cum
- 15% contractor's profit and overheads: ₹1,168.20 per cum
- 1% cess: ₹89.56 per cum

For the basics of how a DSR is laid out, see [CPWD DSR 2023 explained](/blog/cpwd-dsr-2023-explained).

## Pricing it for your own tender

The DSR rate is a Delhi figure at April 2023 prices. Your real cost depends on your own location, lead and carriage, site conditions and any price changes since then. In this build-up:

- Cement is the largest single input, followed by Beldar labour, so your own cement quote and local labour rates both feed straight into your cost.
- Labour is 37.0% of the base cost, so local labour rates matter.
- Lead and carriage change what you pay for materials delivered to site, and they also touch the sundries line, which includes carriage.
- Water charges, GST, profit and overheads, and cess are add-ons on top of the base cost, so check how your own tender treats them.

A simple way to check your own figure:

1. List every input in the analysis: cement, sand, both aggregates, labour, machinery and sundries.
2. Replace each printed rate with your own quote or your own labour rate for the same quantity.
3. Add your own carriage and wastage allowances.
4. Compare your total with the DSR rate and with CPWD's cost before profit and overheads, shown below.

## When a quote drops toward CPWD's cost

Before the 15% contractor's profit and overheads and the 1% cess, CPWD's cost is ₹7,787.99 per cum. That is 86.1% of the DSR rate. This is CPWD's own cost build-up, not a guaranteed loss point. Each contractor's real costs differ.

A quote below ₹7,787.99 is below CPWD's own cost build-up. Before going that low, check your own material quotes, labour rates, lead and wastage. Do not rely on the DSR figures alone.

## What Kerala tenders quoted for this item

170 tenders in Kerala in our database list this exact DSR item with their published results. These are tender-quoted rates, not market prices. They describe past results, not a recommended rate.

- In the middle half of those tenders, the typical quoted rate (the median of each tender's bidders) ran from ₹9,364.22 to ₹10,458.01 per cum. That is 4% above DSR to 16% above DSR 2023.
- The middle value across those tenders was ₹9,745.94 per cum, which is 8% above DSR 2023.
- The lowest (L1) bids for this item had a middle value of ₹9,634.13 per cum, which is 7% above DSR 2023.
- In 4% of those tenders, the typical quote was below ₹7,787.99, CPWD's cost before profit and overheads.

How this item compares with others in the same Kerala tender data: RCC 1:1.5:3 beams and slabs (DSR 5.3) is typically quoted 8% above DSR 2023 across 85 tenders. PCC 1:2:4 is 10% above across 164 tenders. Earthwork excavation in foundation trenches is 26% above across 187 tenders. Shuttering for lintels and beams is typically 5% below across 133 tenders, and steel reinforcement (TMT Fe-500D) is 4% below across 216 tenders. So this item is usually quoted above DSR, like the other concrete work listed, while shuttering and steel are usually quoted below it. For earthwork rates, see [earthwork excavation rate per cum](/blog/earthwork-excavation-rate-per-cum-dsr-2023), and for shuttering, see [centering and shuttering rate per sqm](/blog/shuttering-rate-per-sqm-lintels-beams-dsr-2023).

## Caveats to keep in mind

- These are rates quoted in published tender results. They are not market prices and not a recommended rate.
- The comparison is with the printed DSR 2023 rate, which is at Delhi April 2023 prices. Kerala estimates may add a cost index and local conditions. Older tenders may have used an earlier DSR.
- Each bidder's rate includes what they allowed for lead, site conditions and risk.
